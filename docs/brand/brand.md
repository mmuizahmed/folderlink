# FolderLink brand

Tagline: Your folders. Your AI.

## Mark

The mark is a geometric F in off-white locked with a green block. Together they read as a folder. Draw it from `logo-mark.svg`. Do not trace the brand-sheet photograph.

| Surface | F         | Block     |
| ------- | --------- | --------- |
| Dark    | `#F2F4F7` | `#22C55E` |
| Light   | `#111113` | `#22C55E` |

On a dark app icon, place the mark on `#111113` with even padding. The browser icon is `favicon.svg`.

## Wordmark

`FolderLink` is set in Inter Regular. One word, capital F and capital L. The lockups are `logo-lockup.svg` (dark) and `logo-lockup-light.svg`.

## Color

| Name      | Hex       | Use                                     |
| --------- | --------- | --------------------------------------- |
| Off-white | `#F2F4F7` | Mark on dark, wordmark on dark          |
| Green     | `#22C55E` | The block, and status on the dark panel |
| Ink       | `#111113` | Mark and wordmark on light              |
| Night     | `#09090b` | Panel background                        |

## Clear space

Leave at least the width of the F's stem around the mark. Do not recolor the block, add a shadow, or set the wordmark in a monospace face.
